# Code of Conduct

OpenLifeModel touches health, mortality and personal habits, topics people care
about deeply. We want this to be a welcoming place to discuss them carefully.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Argue about evidence with evidence: cite studies, show your working.
- Remember that real people's health and lives sit behind every number.
- Welcome newcomers and questions at every level.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Medical advice directed at individuals, or shaming people for their health or habits.
- Spam, or promoting products under the guise of discussion.
- Sharing other people's personal or health information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions, and temporarily or
permanently block participants, when this code is not followed. To report a
problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or ask
a maintainer for a private channel in a Discussion. Reports are handled
confidentially.
